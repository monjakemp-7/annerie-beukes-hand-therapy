About the practice

The hand is complex,
and essential to everything you do.

Whether it's cooking a meal, working on the factory floor, typing at a desk or catching a ball, our hands carry us through daily life. When an injury or condition gets in the way, we help you get that function back.

Established in September 2000, Annerie Beukes has spent over 25 years providing specialised hand therapy to a wide variety of clients, from traumatic workplace injuries and fractures to tendon and nerve injuries, amputations, burns, and chronic conditions such as carpal tunnel syndrome, tennis and golfer's elbow, and arthritis. Whether you're recovering from an injury, surgery, or managing a chronic condition, our goal is to help you regain function and get back to what matters to you.

What we treat

Our services

01

Custom Splinting

Upper-extremity splints to immobilise joints after injury, correct deformities and improve function.

02

Oedema Management

Compression therapy, taping, contrast bathing and mobilisation to aid recovery and reduce discomfort.

03

Wound Care

Most hand injuries come with a wound, and treating it is part of treating the injury itself.

04

Burn Rehabilitation

Supporting physical and emotional recovery after a burn, to ease pain and regain function and comfort.

05

Pressure Garments

Custom-made garments for burn scarring, amputations, traumatic hand injuries and chronic nerve pain.

06

Scar Management

Silicone gel dressings, pressure garments, massage and desensitisation to improve appearance and flexibility.

07

Nerve Injury & Re-education

Desensitisation, nerve glides, massage and splinting to support recovering and regenerating nerves.

08

Work Readiness

Close support through recovery from work injuries, strengthening hands and upper limbs for a safe return to work.

09

Taping

Supporting muscles and joints and facilitating natural healing, without restricting range of motion.

10

Red Light Therapy

Low-level red light therapy for the hand, used to ease pain, calm inflammation and support tissue healing.

Made in-house

Every splint and garment, custom-made by hand.

From thermoplastic splints to pressure garments, everything is measured, cut and stitched in-house, giving you a precise, comfortable fit made specifically for your hand.
